How Penalties Work

1
Base target: 6h of FOCUS. Break NEVER counts.
2
Complete → Target stays same. Streak +1. ✅
3
Miss → +1h penalty tomorrow. Streak resets. ❌
4
Max target: 15h total. Never more.
5
Min target: Your base (default 6h).
6
Reduction: Every 2 extra hours → -0.5h penalty.
7
Overtime: After target hit, timer counts UP. +150 XP/h.
