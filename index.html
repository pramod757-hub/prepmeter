📜 Penalty Rules

How the penalty system works

Penalty System Rules

1
Base daily target: 6 hours of FOCUS time. Break time NEVER counts.
2
Complete target → Tomorrow stays same base target. Streak +1. ✅
3
Miss target → Tomorrow gets +1h penalty. Streak resets to 0. ❌
4
Maximum target: 15 hours (base + penalty). Never goes above this.
5
Minimum target: Your base target (default 6h). Never goes below.
6
Penalty reduction: Every 2 EXTRA overtime hours → penalty reduces by 0.5 hours.
7
Overtime mode: After target is hit, timer continues counting UP. Every extra hour earns +150 XP bonus.
